The personalized content, data files, responsive stylesheet, SVG illustrations,
and navigation script were prepared for Yibo Liu's homepage. The illustrations
are thematic graphics rather than figures extracted from the publications.

Publication titles, author order, journal names, and DOI identifiers were checked
against Crossref on 2026-10-09. Summaries, author roles, education, projects, and
awards are based on the previous homepage. Ph.D. enrollment status was confirmed
by the owner during this revision.
